# Product Requirements (KPSMS)

## Core
- Employee profile, attendance, payroll, leave, approvals, config.
- Admin/Supervisor/Staff roles.

## Employee Pay Structure
- Each employee has individually configurable Daily Salary and Daily Duty Hours.
- Hourly Rate = Daily Salary / Daily Duty Hours.
- Partial attendance paid proportionally.

## Flexible Shifts
- Admin-customizable shift templates.
- Employee not permanently tied to one shift.
- Admin/Supervisor can assign different shifts per date.
- Meal and rest breaks included in shift duration; not deducted.

## Flexible Departments
- Editable departments; historical assignments traceable.
- Employees can have multiple responsibilities.
- Deactivate rather than hard-delete used departments.

## Responsibilities
- All except Cash Counter share general customer service.
- Additional responsibilities separately assignable.

## Historical Snapshots
- Attendance/payroll preserve salary/duty-hour/shift snapshots.
