# Employee, Department and Additional Responsibilities

- Employee profile separate from department/shift/responsibility.
- Departments editable; historical assignments traceable.
- Employee may have multiple department responsibilities.
- Used departments deactivated, not hard-deleted.
- All except Cash Counter share general customer service.
- Additional responsibilities separately assignable and changeable:
  - Medicine order placement
  - Veterinary medicine orders
  - Agricultural poison and seed orders
  - Consumer product orders
  - Product receiving and quantity checking
  - Rack arrangement and stock organization
  - Expiry checking and shortage reporting
